Enough to not need to count
Why searches are counted and nothing else is. A search runs a live AI query across real retailer listings, and that costs us real money every time. Fit checks, colour analysis and your shopping guide cost us almost nothing, so they are not rationed. We would rather be straightforward about that than pretend the limits are arbitrary.

What counts as a search

One search is one set of recommendations: you tell us the occasion, the weather and your budget, and we come back with pieces that suit your shape, your colouring and your height. Looking again at results you have already been given, saving them, or reading your shopping guide does not count.

When the month resets

Your allowance resets on the same date each month, the day your subscription renews. Unused searches do not roll over.

If you run out

Nothing breaks and nothing is charged automatically. You keep everything you have already been given and can still use fit checks, your profile and your shopping guide. You can move up a plan whenever you like, or wait for the reset.

Changing or cancelling

Move between plans whenever you want. Moving up takes effect straight away; moving down takes effect at your next renewal, so you keep what you have paid for.

You can cancel at any time from your account, in a couple of taps. See our refunds and cancellation policy for when we give money back — the short version is fourteen days, no hard feelings.

If prices change

We may change our prices from time to time, and we will always email you at least 30 days before a new price applies to you, so you can decide whether to carry on. A price change never touches a month you have already paid for, or a gift. The detail is in our terms.

Tax

Where tax applies where you live, it is shown before you pay and included in the total at checkout.
