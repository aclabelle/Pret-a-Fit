Privacy policy

Your privacy,
plainly stated.

We believe privacy policies should be readable. Here is ours, written in plain language.

Last updated: November 2026

What we collect

When you use Prêt-à-Fit, you provide us with style preferences — your body type, height range, sizing, location, occasion, colour preferences and style inspirations. If you choose to, you can also tell us the month a baby is due, which is covered separately below. This information is used solely to generate clothing recommendations for you. We also collect your email address if you create an account or contact us.

How we use it

Your preferences are passed to our AI provider (Anthropic) to generate personalised recommendations. They are not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than producing your results. If you create an account, your profile preferences are saved so you don't need to re-enter them each visit.

What we don't do

We do not sell your data. We do not share your information with retailers or third-party advertisers. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles. We do not store payment information — this is handled entirely by Paddle, our payment provider.

If you tell us you're expecting

This one is optional, and we will never ask you for it. There is a field you can fill in if you want to, and if you leave it alone we won't mention it again.  If you do use it, we store one thing: the month a baby is due. Not a date, not a history, nothing about your health beyond that. We work out roughly how far along you are from today's date, so that what we recommend suits the stage you're at rather than going stale.  We treat this as health information, which in the UK and Europe carries extra protection, and that is why it is opt-in, why we keep as little as we can, and why nothing else in the app refers to it.  You can clear it in one tap, from your profile, with no confirmation box and nothing asking you why. Once it's gone it's gone, and we go back to recommending as we did before.  After the month you gave us has passed, we simply stop applying it. We don't assume anything about how a pregnancy went, and we will never start recommending baby clothes or nursing wear off the back of a date.

Photo analysis (coming soon)

When we introduce optional photo-based body assessment, any image you upload will be sent directly to our AI provider for analysis and immediately discarded. Photos are never stored, never shared, and never used for any purpose beyond the single analysis you request.

Gifts

If you buy a gift, we keep the first name you give for the person it's for, their email address if you ask us to send it to them, and your message, alongside the gift on your account. We use them only to deliver the gift and to show it to you, never for marketing, and we don't add the person to any list. If you're given a gift, we keep who it was from (their first name) and the dates it covers on your account, so we can show it on your profile.

Cookies

We use minimal, functional cookies only — to keep you logged in and remember your preferences between sessions. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Most of it you can change or clear yourself from your profile at any time, including the due month if you've set one. To exercise these rights, or if you have any questions about how your data is handled, please contact us at support@pret-a-fit.com

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will notify registered users by email. The date at the top of this page always reflects when it was last updated.

Payments

When you subscribe, your payment is handled by Paddle, our payment provider, who acts as the seller of record. Your card details go to Paddle and never to us — we never see or store a card number. Paddle holds the billing information needed to take payment and meet tax obligations, under its own privacy policy. We keep only what we need to know which plan you are on: your plan, its status, and its renewal date.
