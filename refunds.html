Refunds & cancellation

If it isn't right,
tell us.

We would rather give you your money back than keep a subscriber who isn't getting anything from this. Here is exactly how that works, in plain language.

Last updated: November 2026

Try it before you pay

Every visitor gets a complimentary search and three free fit checks without entering a card. We would much rather you used those first and decided from there. Nobody should be buying this blind, and most refund requests exist because somebody had to. Our pricing page sets out what each plan includes.

Your first fourteen days

If you change your mind within fourteen days of your first payment, write to us and we will refund you in full — provided you have run five searches or fewer. No explanation needed and no hard feelings.

If you have run more than five searches in that time, we will refund the unused remainder of the month rather than the whole of it. We are not being difficult: every search is a live AI query that costs us real money to run, and a full refund after heavy use would mean the searches cost us more than you paid. Five is deliberately generous — it is five times what a free visitor gets.

After fourteen days

You can cancel whenever you like, and you keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for. We do not refund part-months after the first fourteen days, and we do not charge you again once you have cancelled.

Things we always refund

Charged twice for the same month, charged after you cancelled, charged the wrong amount, or the service was unavailable for a sustained period — all refunded in full, whenever they happen and whether or not you are inside the first fourteen days. If we got the billing wrong, that is ours to put right.

A renewal you didn't expect

If a subscription renewed and you had meant to cancel, write to us. If you haven't used the new period, we will almost always refund it. We would rather do that than have you feel caught out — and we send reminders before renewals, before every one unless you've chosen to hear from us twice a year, precisely so this doesn't happen.

Changing plans

Moving up a plan takes effect straight away and you pay the difference for the rest of the month. Moving down takes effect at your next renewal, so you keep what you have already paid for rather than losing it.

What we don't refund

We can't refund a month you have used extensively and then decided wasn't to your taste, and we can't refund anything you bought from a retailer — those purchases are between you and them, under their own returns policy. We also can't refund on the basis that a recommendation didn't suit you: the advice is honest, considered and sometimes wrong, which is why we tell you to trust your own eye in the mirror.

How to cancel

You can cancel at any time from your account, in a couple of taps, without writing to anyone or explaining yourself. If you would rather just email us, support@pret-a-fit.com works too and we will do it for you.

How refunds reach you

Refunds go back to the card you paid with — we cannot send them anywhere else. We action them within three working days of agreeing one, and your bank usually takes another five to ten to show it.

Your rights where you live

Nothing here takes away a right you have under the consumer law of your own country. If you live somewhere that gives you a stronger cancellation right than this policy does — much of the UK and Europe does, for instance — that right stands, and you are welcome to rely on it instead. If the two ever conflict, whichever is better for you is the one that applies.

If you're unhappy with our answer

Tell us, and a person will look at it again properly. We are a very small company and there is no refund department to escalate through — but there is somebody who reads every message and would rather fix something than argue about it.
